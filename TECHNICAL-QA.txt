NUTRIMATCH - TECHNICAL Q&A CHEAT SHEET
For answering panel / adviser questions. Short answer first, then the detail.
(Updated Oct 14, 2026 - matches the code on main.)

======================================================================
1. PAYMENTS (PayMongo)
======================================================================

Q: How does a client pay?
A: Through PayMongo, a licensed Philippine payment gateway. When the RND
   confirms a booking, the system issues an invoice. The client presses
   "Pay now", our backend creates a PayMongo Checkout Session, and the
   client pays on PayMongo's secure page with QR Ph, GCash, Maya or card.
   We never see or store card numbers - PayMongo handles that.

Q: When does the client pay?
A: Before the consultation. The invoice is created when the RND confirms,
   and the client can pay any time before the scheduled start.
   - Until it's paid, the client gets NO video link (the Join button
     is hidden and the server doesn't send the room link).
   - If it's still unpaid at the start time, the system automatically
     cancels the appointment, voids the invoice, and notifies both sides.

Q: How does the system know the client paid?
A: Two ways, so a payment is never missed:
   1. Webhook - PayMongo calls our endpoint (/api/webhooks/paymongo/) the
      moment the payment succeeds. We verify PayMongo's HMAC signature
      first, so fake "paid" messages are rejected.
   2. Sync - the Billing page also asks PayMongo directly for the status
      of unpaid invoices (on page load and when the tab is focused).
   Both use the same function (record_successful_payment), so a payment is
   recorded once, even if both arrive.

Q: Is it real money?
A: For the demo we use PayMongo's TEST mode (sandbox). The flow is identical
   to live; switching to live only needs the live API keys (PayMongo first
   verifies the business).

Q: How can you PROVE a payment went through PayMongo?
A: Every receipt shows PayMongo's own payment ID (pay_...). Open the PayMongo
   dashboard (Test mode) -> Payments and search that ID: the same amount,
   method, time and "NutriMatch Consultation Invoice #N" are there, plus
   PayMongo's fee. The admin invoice pop-up also has "Check with PayMongo",
   which asks PayMongo live and shows its answer.

Q: What happens after paying?
A: The invoice becomes "Paid", a payment transaction is logged, an audit log
   row is written, and a branded receipt is emailed to the client (copy to
   the NutriMatch inbox). The client can also download/print the receipt or
   press "Resend receipt".

======================================================================
2. CANCELLATIONS AND REFUNDS
======================================================================

Q: What if the client or RND cancels?
A: - Not yet paid: the appointment is simply cancelled; nothing to refund.
   - Already paid: the invoice becomes "Refund pending" (the client sees
     "Refund in progress").
   The client can cancel pending or confirmed appointments; the RND must
   give a reason (at least 10 characters) when declining or cancelling, and
   the client is notified with that reason.

Q: How is the refund done?
A: Through PayMongo, started by the admin:
   1. Billing & Commission -> Refunds shows "Refunds to send".
   2. Admin presses "Refund through PayMongo" and confirms. NutriMatch sends
      the refund to PayMongo's Refunds API (in full, never twice).
   3. The invoice becomes "Refunded" ONLY when PayMongo reports the refund
      "succeeded" - then the refund ID (ref_...) is saved, an audit log row
      is written and the client is notified. A click alone can't mark it.
   4. GCash / Maya / card refunds go straight back to the same wallet or
      card. QR Ph refunds are claimed by the client through a PayMongo link
      (valid 3 days) to their bank or e-wallet.
   The client is told: refunded within 5-7 business days.
   WHY a human starts it: refunds move money, so the admin decides; but
   PayMongo, not the admin, confirms it happened.

Q: How can you prove the refund happened?
A: PayMongo dashboard (Test mode) -> Payments: the payment's status changes
   to REFUNDED, and its page lists the refund (ref_...) as Succeeded - the
   same ID shown in NutriMatch's admin invoice pop-up ("In PayMongo").

Q: Why does a QR Ph refund fail in the demo?
A: PayMongo's test mode can't complete QR Ph refunds (we tested it: the
   refund is recorded as "failed", and the normal refund API says "Refunds
   are not allowed for payments with source type qrph"). NutriMatch shows
   that answer and keeps the invoice "Refund pending" instead of pretending.
   For the demo, pay with GCash, Maya or a test card to show a refund.

Q: Does the RND still get paid for a refunded consultation?
A: No. Refunded or refund-pending invoices are never paid out to the RND,
   earn no platform commission, and are excluded from the RND's share in
   reports and earnings.

Q: What if a payment arrives AFTER the appointment was cancelled?
A: The system flags that invoice "Refund pending" automatically, so the
   admin refunds it.

======================================================================
3. COMMISSION AND RND PAYOUTS
======================================================================

Q: How does NutriMatch earn money?
A: A platform commission (currently 10%) of each consultation fee.
   Example: fee P700 -> commission P70 -> RND share P630.

Q: Can the commission change for old invoices if the rate changes?
A: No. The commission amount is FROZEN on the invoice when it is created
   (commission_amt). Changing the rate later only affects new invoices.
   This follows the proposal's data design.

Q: How and when are RNDs paid?
A: Monthly, every 5th of the month:
   - Pending: the consultation is paid but not yet completed, or completed
     less than 3 days ago (a 3-day hold in case of disputes/refunds).
   - Posted: 3 days after completion - ready to be paid out.
   - On/after the 5th, the admin releases Posted balances of P500 or more
     to the RND's GCash or bank account (set on the RND's Earnings page).
     Smaller balances carry over to the next month.
   - The transfer itself happens outside the app (GCash/bank); the admin
     records the reference number. The system links each invoice to that
     payout so nothing is paid twice, logs it, and emails the RND.

Q: Can the RND see their earnings?
A: Yes - Earnings page: each invoice with fee - commission = share and its
   state (awaiting payment / pending / posted / paid out / refunded),
   payout history, and a printable statement.

======================================================================
4. BOOKING RULES
======================================================================

Q: What rules does booking follow? (enforced by the SERVER, not just the UI)
A: - At least 10 minutes in the future (no past times).
   - Inside the RND's weekly hours; start times every hour from the start
     of their hours; not during the RND's blocked time off.
   - No overlap with the RND's or the client's other bookings
     (taken times show greyed out as "Booked").
   - The RND must offer that format (video call or in-person); in-person
     needs a clinic address.
   - Client must finish onboarding (health background + screening) first.
   - One RND at a time: a client with an active RND can't book another
     until that RND discharges them.

Q: Flow of a consultation?
A: Book -> RND reviews screening and confirms (or declines with reason) ->
   invoice -> client pays -> room opens 15 min before start, closes 15 min
   after the end -> RND writes the NCP record in the room -> "End & mark
   complete" (only if paid) -> client gets a consultation summary.

Q: How do video calls work?
A: Jitsi Meet (free, no account needed). Each appointment gets a random,
   unguessable room name, and the link is only given to the client after
   payment and inside the join window. Consultations are video call or
   in-person only (chat consultations were removed on our adviser's advice);
   the in-app Messages chat is for questions between consultations.

======================================================================
5. CLINICAL PART (NCP, calculations, meal plans)
======================================================================

Q: What clinical method does the system follow?
A: The Nutrition Care Process (NCP) of the Academy of Nutrition and
   Dietetics: Assessment -> Diagnosis (PES statement: Problem, Etiology,
   Signs/Symptoms) -> Intervention -> Monitoring & Evaluation.
   Stored as ONE longitudinal record per visit (not 4 tables).
   Once finalized, a record is permanent (cannot be edited or deleted).

Q: What formulas are used?
A: - BMI with WHO ASIA-PACIFIC cut-offs (normal 18.5-22.9, overweight
     23-24.9, obese I 25-29.9, obese II >= 30) - not the global WHO ones,
     because they fit Filipino/Asian bodies.
   - BMR: Mifflin-St Jeor equation.
     Male:   10 x kg + 6.25 x cm - 5 x age + 5
     Female: 10 x kg + 6.25 x cm - 5 x age - 161
   - TDEE = BMR x activity factor (1.2 / 1.375 / 1.55 / 1.725 / 1.9).
   - NRS-2002 nutritional risk score (score >= 3 = at risk).
   Example from testing: 74 kg, 158 cm, 31 y/o female, lightly active
   -> BMI 29.6 (Obese I), BMR 1,412 kcal, TDEE 1,941 kcal.

Q: Where does food data come from?
A: FNRI Food Exchange Lists (Philippine) as the main source; USDA
   FoodData Central as a second source. For RA 10173 data minimization we
   only store the FOOD NAME from outside sources, not their full data.

Q: How are diet templates made?
A: 4 templates: Diabetes, Hypertension (DASH), Renal (CKD), Weight
   management. From the target kcal and the condition's macro split, the
   system computes daily FNRI exchanges, then fills 5 meals x 7 days with
   foods allowed for that condition. It skips:
   - foods matching the client's allergies (e.g. "Shrimp / shellfish"
     removes shrimp, prawn, crab, squid...),
   - high-sodium foods for hypertension, high potassium/phosphorus/sodium
     foods for renal.
   The result is a DRAFT - the RND reviews and edits before sending.

Q: What if the RND adds an unsuitable food by hand?
A: The meal plan editor shows a warning under that food (allergy, or e.g.
   "High in sodium - limit for hypertension"), marks the day, and asks
   before sending. It's a warning only - the RND makes the final decision.

Q: How is adherence measured?
A: The client logs each meal (photo and/or note); the RND rates it
   Followed (100%) / Partly (50%) / Not followed (0%). Unlogged planned
   meals count as missed. Goal line is 80%.

Q: Who can see a client's meal photos?
A: Only the client and their own RND. Meal photos are health data (RA
   10173), so they're stored privately on Cloudinary: the photo's normal
   address doesn't work (Cloudinary answers 401). When the client or their
   RND opens a meal log, NutriMatch makes a download link that expires
   after 10 minutes - a copied or forwarded link stops working. Admins and
   other RNDs get no link at all.

======================================================================
6. SECURITY AND DATA PRIVACY (RA 10173)
======================================================================

Q: How do you comply with the Data Privacy Act (RA 10173)?
A: - Informed consent at sign-up (two required checkboxes, time-stamped and
     logged).
   - Role-based access: client / RND / admin each see only what they need.
     An RND sees only their own patients; clients never see RND-only notes
     or other people's contact details.
   - Audit logs: append-only records of sensitive actions (viewing health
     records, edits, refunds, payouts, consent) - who, what, when.
   - Data minimization (only food names from outside APIs).
   - Passwords are hashed - nobody, not even admins, can see them; admins
     can only send a password reset.
   - Encryption in transit (HTTPS) when deployed.

Q: How do users log in securely?
A: Email + password with JWT tokens (expire after 1 hour, refresh tokens
   rotate). Email must be verified with a 6-digit code first. Strong
   passwords required (upper, lower, number, special). Login and sign-up are
   rate-limited against brute force.

Q: How are RNDs verified?
A: They upload their PRC license photo + 7-digit license number at sign-up.
   An admin checks it (against the PRC website) and approves or rejects
   with a reason. Unapproved RNDs cannot sign in. License photos are stored
   privately and shown to admins only through temporary signed links.

======================================================================
7. TECHNOLOGY
======================================================================

Q: What is the system built with? (short answer)
A: Nuxt 4 (Vue 3) frontend + Django 6 / Django REST Framework backend,
   talking through a REST API with JWT login. SQLite in development,
   PostgreSQL for deployment. PayMongo for payments, Jitsi for video,
   Cloudinary for files, Gmail SMTP for emails.

Q: Architecture?
A: Client-server, "decoupled":
   Browser (Nuxt app)  --HTTPS + JSON (REST API, JWT token)-->  Django API
   Django API  -->  Database (SQLite dev / PostgreSQL prod)
   Django API  -->  outside services (PayMongo, Cloudinary, Gmail, USDA)
   PayMongo    -->  Django webhook (payment confirmed)
   The frontend never touches the database directly; every rule (booking,
   payment, permissions) is checked again on the server.

Q: How do you protect the API?
A: - JWT required on every endpoint except sign-up/login and the PayMongo
     webhook (which is verified by PayMongo's signature instead).
   - Role permissions per group (/client, /rnd, /admin) + ownership checks
     (an RND can only open their own patients; a client only their own
     records - others get 404).
   - All input is validated on the server (ranges, dates, file types and
     sizes), not just in the form.
   - Rate limits on login, sign-up, email codes and password reset.

Q: Why Django instead of Laravel (from the proposal)?
A: Team decision during development - Django REST Framework gave us
   built-in security (auth, permissions, validation), an admin-friendly ORM,
   and easy testing (400+ automated tests). Same features and data design
   as the proposal.

Q: Do you send SMS?
A: Yes, through Semaphore, a Philippine SMS gateway (all networks). Clients
   turn it on in Notification settings (off by default, needs a mobile
   number). To keep the cost low only four things are texted, each one short
   SMS with no health details (Data Privacy Act):
     1. Booking confirmed - please pay before the consultation.
     2. Payment due (still unpaid within 24 h of the start).
     3. Consultation starts in 1 hour.
     4. The RND (or NutriMatch) cancelled the consultation.
   Everything else (24-hour reminder, tasks, meal logging, labs) stays
   in-app / email. About P0.50-0.60 per text: roughly 2-4 texts per
   consultation. A failed text never stops the in-app or email reminder.

Q: How do reminders work?
A: Automatic notifications: 24 h and 1 h before a consultation, payment due,
   meal logging after each planned meal, and RND-set reminders (medicine,
   habits). The client sees them as tasks on the dashboard.

Q: How was it tested?
A: 400+ automated backend tests, plus full manual end-to-end tests:
   sign-up -> approval -> booking -> real PayMongo test payment ->
   consultation -> NCP -> meal plan -> refund -> admin reports.
