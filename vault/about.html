ABOUT NUTRIMATCH

Modernizing Medical
Nutrition Therapy in the Philippines

A capstone project by BSIT Group 12, STI College Davao — built to close the gap between Filipino RNDs and the patients who need structured nutrition care.

Our Mission

To give every licensed RND in the Philippines a digital practice — one that replaces paper charting with structured, auditable, FNRI-grounded clinical workflows, so Medical Nutrition Therapy can reach more Filipinos managing diabetes, hypertension, renal disease, and obesity.

Why It Matters

Noncommunicable diseases account for roughly 3 in 4 deaths worldwide, and the Philippines faces a dual burden of malnutrition — persistent stunting alongside rising obesity. NutriMatch operationalizes the WHO-recommended Nutrition Care Process to make evidence-based dietary care more accessible.
