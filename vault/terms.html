Terms & Conditions

Last updated: June 2026

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account on NutriMatch, you agree to be bound by these Terms & Conditions. NutriMatch is a web-based clinical nutrition consultation management system connecting clients with licensed Registered Nutritionist-Dietitians (RNDs) in the Philippines.

2. Eligibility & Roles

NutriMatch operates under three roles: Client, RND, and Administrator. RND accounts require a valid, unexpired PRC (Professional Regulation Commission) license number, which is manually verified by an administrator before clinical features become accessible. Clinical features — including the Nutrition Care Process, meal planning, and progress monitoring — are only accessible within a formally established, active RND-client relationship.

3. Nature of the Service

NutriMatch provides tools to support, not replace, professional clinical judgment. Automated computations including BMI, BMR, TDEE, and NRS-2002 nutritional risk scoring are intended as decision-support aids. Final clinical decisions remain the responsibility of the supervising RND. NutriMatch is not a substitute for emergency medical care.

4. Payments & Billing

Consultation fees are set independently by each RND and displayed on their profile prior to booking. Payments are processed through PayMongo, a third-party payment provider. NutriMatch retains a platform commission on completed transactions, frozen at the rate in effect at the time of the transaction.

5. User Conduct

  • You agree to provide accurate registration and clinical information
  • RNDs agree to maintain valid professional credentials for the duration of platform use
  • You agree not to misuse messaging, video, or resource-sharing features for purposes unrelated to nutrition care
  • You agree not to attempt to access clinical records outside an established care relationship

6. Service Availability

NutriMatch requires a stable internet connection; no offline mode is available. Video conferencing, payment processing, and SMS notifications depend on third-party providers and are subject to their respective uptime and service terms.

7. Limitation of Liability

NutriMatch is provided as an academic capstone project. While built to professional standards, it is not formally registered as a Data Processing System with the National Privacy Commission. Institutions or individuals deploying NutriMatch for live clinical use assume responsibility for regulatory compliance appropriate to their context.
