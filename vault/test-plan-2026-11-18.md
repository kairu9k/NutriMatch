# Pre-launch test plan: registration to finish (2026-11-18)

**Goal:** before `rnd-design-pass` goes to `main` (Render + Vercel deploy on push), walk one RND and one client through the whole app the way real people will, from sign-up to the RND's payout, with extra weight on what changed on Nov 16 (consultation closing, no-show, disputes in Help & Support, rescheduling). Every bug found is logged, traced to its cause and fixed, with a test where the backend is involved.

**Where:** a fresh scratch database (`scratch.sqlite3`, only an admin and the FNRI seed to start), backend on 127.0.0.1:8001, Nuxt on 127.0.0.1:3001, a new Chrome tab. The dev DB, the site on 3000 and Neon are not touched. Emails print to the backend log (Gmail and Brevo blank, so sign-up codes can be read there); SMS off; PayMongo test mode; real Cloudinary uploads (PRC photo, selfie, lab file, meal photo), deleted at the end.

**Time shortcuts:** booking needs 45 minutes' notice and a consultation lasts an hour, so a booked consultation's time is moved with the Django shell to reach "starts now", "15 min in" or "ended". Only the clock is moved; everything else goes through the app.

**Findings:** `vault/test-findings-2026-11-18.md` (what, where, cause, fix, proof).

## 2. Fresh test world

## 3. RND journey
1. Register as an RND (3 steps: account and licence with PRC photo, selfie, specializations, then review). Email code from the log, then "Application submitted".
2. Sign-in is refused while pending.
3. Admin: RND applications. Licence and selfie show, approve only after the face check is ticked. Approval email.
4. RND signs in, lands on onboarding: profile, consultations (fee, video + in-person, clinic address), weekly hours, payout. Finish setup lands on the dashboard with the welcome pop-up.
5. The RND now appears in Find an RND.

## 4. Client journey
1. Register as a client, email code, onboarding (about you, health background with an allergy, screening with results), Finish setup lands on the dashboard with the welcome pop-up.
2. Find an RND, open the profile, book a video consultation (calendar, times, note).
3. RND: Appointments, Requests, confirm. Invoice created, client told.
4. Client pays through PayMongo test mode; Billing syncs it to paid; receipt.

## 5. Video consultation, completed by itself (new)
- Move the time to "starting now". Both join: RND in one tab on 127.0.0.1:3001, client in another on localhost:3001 (separate sign-ins). Camera/mic permission may need you to click Allow once.
- Check: the call connects, attendance is recorded, the RND's room panels (Patient, NCP, lab request), the 15/5 min warnings and the gold timer, the "Ended" cut-off at the scheduled end.
- When the room closes: completed by itself (≥ 5 min together), the client gets the summary, the RND's earnings show it on hold, no Mark complete needed.
- After: NCP record written in the room and finalized, meal plan sent (allergy warning), client logs a meal with a photo, RND rates it, lab request then upload then accept, review.

## 6. The other ways a consultation ends (new)
Each with a fresh booking, same pay path (paid through the shell after the first real PayMongo payment):
1. **No-show:** the RND waits, the client never joins. "Mark a no-show" is locked until 15 min after the start, then works. Client sees the no-show and the "Use Help & Support, Dispute a consultation" line.
2. **Dispute:** the client disputes it in Help & Support (picker only lists disputable ones, 20+ characters). Payout held. Admin Support: refund or keep; both sides told.
3. **Nobody came / call failed:** the room closes, refund pending, admin refunds through PayMongo (GCash test refund).
4. **RND absent:** the client joins and waits 15 min, reports the RND didn't come, gets a refund, the RND late cancel is counted.
5. **Reschedule:** the RND cancels a paid consultation, offers a later time (earlier ones not offered), the client proposes another, the RND accepts. Same invoice, new time, reminders.
6. **In-person:** the RND marks it complete from Open room; the client can dispute for 3 days; left open 3+ days shows on admin Needs attention.

## 7. Money to the end
- Move `completed_at` back past the 3-day hold: the RND's share turns Cleared. Admin payout run, release with a reference. RND Earnings shows Paid out, payout email in the log.
- Platform reports and the admin dashboard count it right (refunded money earns no commission).
